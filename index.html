Reference Guide

Definitions for every term used in the calculator.

Tent Types

Gable-End Tent

The two short ends of the tent are vertical flat walls. The roof slopes only on the two long sides. Ceiling fabric covers 2 rectangular slope panels.

Hip-End Tent

All four sides slope down from the peak — including the short ends. There are no vertical end walls. Ceiling fabric covers 2 long rectangular panels plus 2 triangular hip end panels.

Dimensions
Tent LengthInput
The longer dimension of the tent footprint, in feet.
Tent WidthInput
The shorter dimension of the tent footprint, in feet.
Eave HeightInput
Height from the ground to the top of the side wall — the point where the roof begins to slope.
Peak HeightInput
Height from the ground to the highest point of the tent roof.
Hip ExtensionHip-End Only
The horizontal distance the roof extends outward on each short end. Must come from the customer or tent schematic.
Calculated Values
RiseCalculated
The vertical distance from the eave up to the peak. This is the key driver of the slope calculation.
Rise = Peak Height − Eave Height
Main Slope LengthCalculated
The length of a long roof panel from eave to peak, calculated using the Pythagorean theorem.
Slope = √( Rise² + (Width ÷ 2)² )
Hip Slope LengthCalculated
The slope length of the triangular hip end panels. Uses the same rise but the run is the hip extension distance.
Hip Slope = √( Rise² + Hip Extension² )
Fabric Settings
Fabric WidthInput
The width of the fabric roll being used. Common widths are 5 ft (60") and 10 ft (120"). Determines how many cuts per leg are needed.
Fullness FactorInput
How much extra fabric is gathered to create a draped look. Applied to ceiling area only — not legs.
1.0× = Flat · 1.5× = 50% · 2.0× = 100% · 3.0× = 200%
30% Roll Yield BufferCeiling
Added to ceiling yardage to account for cut inefficiency. When cuts don't divide evenly into roll lengths, partial rolls get used up — this buffer covers that loss.
10% Leg BufferLegs
Added to leg drape yardage to account for waste and cut allowance. Smaller than the ceiling buffer because leg cuts are shorter and more predictable.
Leg Drapes
Number of LegsInput
Perimeter poles only — not interior supports. Always provided by the customer or tent schematic.
Drape HeightCalculated
The cut length for each leg drape panel. Always includes a 6" pocket allowance at the top.
Drape Height = Eave Height + 0.5 ft
Drapes Per LegInput
How many fabric widths are needed to wrap each pole. Depends on fabric width.
Fabric < 10 ft wide → 2 cuts per leg
Fabric ≥ 10 ft wide → 1 cut per leg
Final Recommendations
Ceiling RecommendationCalculated
Total yards for the roof liner. Includes fullness factor and 30% roll yield buffer. Rounded up to the nearest whole yard.
Leg RecommendationCalculated
Total yards for all pole drapes. Includes 10% buffer. Rounded up to the nearest whole yard.
Total RecommendationCalculated
Ceiling Recommendation + Leg Recommendation. This is the number to quote the customer.
Total = Ceiling Rec. + Leg Rec.
